Project 01 · Final year case study

ConceptIdentify

AI-Assisted Educational Platform

A full-stack system helping lecturers identify difficult concepts and shape assessments around them.

01The problem

Make difficult concepts visible.

ConceptIdentify is designed around a practical teaching problem: helping lecturers inspect learning material and identify which concepts need more attention.

02The system

One platform, with a reviewable workflow.

The verified project stack includes Django, Next.js, and PostgreSQL, connected through REST APIs with authentication.
